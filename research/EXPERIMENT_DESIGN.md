## Data separation and statistics
No native development split was established by this source audit. Test data are used for qualification/diagnosis only, never parameter/prompt choice, label retrieval, fine-tuning, neuron discovery or best-of-layer selection. Legitimate non-test development and fresh native confirmation are prerequisites for a future adaptive method.

Report finite-release descriptive values first. CB unit=question group; BLEnD unit=global original template ID, preserving all culture/language/prompt/MCQ variants. For uncertainty over a declared template population, a trusted live callback must perform paired cluster resampling and recompute macro/micro/SEM estimands from raw native outcomes. Such a callback is not implemented or qualified in this prelude. Do not reuse a generic independent-row confidence interval or a saved statistical verdict.

There are two predeclared categorical arms and a fixed two-prompt diagnosis, not a searched family of winning variants. No post-hoc inferential significance claim. Future candidate family-wise tests require a frozen multiplicity plan (e.g. Holm over the declared primary model × benchmark contrasts), paired estimands, effect margin justified by actual consequence, and a sample/precision calculation from independent development evidence. Native 1,227 CB clusters and 500 BLEnD templates impose real precision limits; millions of repeated rows do not increase independent N.

No universal “three seeds” rule. Greedy decoding is deterministic conditional on implementation; repeated identical seeds do not estimate item uncertainty. Future stochastic/learned methods need repetitions for their actual variance components. Multiple models in different families do not establish a causal scaling law.
